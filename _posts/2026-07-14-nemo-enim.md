The home calendar is a stack of **sticky beats**, not a wall of cards. Open a note when you need the extra pass. The countdown in the hero is aimed at **1 September**.

## What the beats cover

Fork day, journal, four themes, the accessibility pass, and launch morning.

- Tableau plus Corex, no npm
- Journal pagination on the client
- Accessibility choices persist in local storage

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Change the target in the home template when you have a real date. Days, hours, minutes, and seconds stay on one row.

The header timer stays in the bar and appears after the hero[^orbit].

[^orbit]: The condensed header and progress bar stay as chrome when motion is reduced.
