Launch pages often drown visitors in a twelve-month roadmap nobody asked for. Soonex trims the story to **four beats**—each dated, each scannable—leading to a single public launch. The home band mirrors what a founder would actually say out loud: waitlist open, skins ready, journal live, ship date.

## Why four beats

A long calendar widget competes with the waitlist. Four cards fit above the fold on desktop and stack cleanly on mobile. Each beat can be featured with `data-featured="true"` styling so the current milestone reads louder without animation.

The beats are content, not config. Edit copy in `Soonex.HomePage.LaunchBand` when your story changes. Only the **target date** is shared infrastructure.

## One module for the countdown

The public launch moment lives in `Soonex.Launch`:

- `@target` — UTC datetime for 1 December 2026 in the demo
- `year_label/0` — human-readable label for hero badge and stats
- `countdown_ms/0` — milliseconds until launch for the header timer

Hero badge and header timer both read these functions. Change the date once; do not scatter `DateTime.diff` across templates.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Change the date in one module. Skins, hero, header, and launch band stay in sync automatically.

## Header timer behaviour

The countdown in the nav uses Corex `<.timer>` with compact digits and **d h m s** unit labels. It stays invisible and inert until the visitor scrolls past the hero sentinel—then it fades in without shifting header height. That avoids the “sticky bar jumps when numbers appear” problem on long hero sections.

Reduced motion keeps the timer visible but disables ornamental transitions. Digits still update every second.

## What each beat represents in the demo

| Beat | Demo meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Waitlist | Forms and toast path ready |
| Skins | Four themes shippable from CSS |
| Journal | Blog, tags, pagination wired |
| Launch | Public date shown in hero and timer |

Replace these labels with your product milestones: private beta, design partners, pricing page, GA—whatever matches your go-to-market.

## Stats band alignment

The stats section under the hero echoes the same launch year so numbers and countdown never contradict. If you change `@target` to a different year, update stat copy in `Soonex.HomePage.Stats` in the same commit.

The public launch beat is **1 December 2026** until you replace it[^orbit].

[^orbit]: `Soonex.Launch.year_label/0` is what the hero and stats band print.

## Local preview

```bash
mix server
```

Scroll the home page: hero badge shows the year label, header timer appears after the hero, launch band lists four beats with the final beat featured. Switch themes—the date does not move because it is not skin-local.

## After you fork

1. Set `@target` to your ship date.
2. Rewrite beat titles and descriptions.
3. Update journal posts so the narrative matches your timeline.
4. Announce when the waitlist opens for real—not when the template demo loads.

A credible launch page tells time in one voice. Soonex keeps that voice in one module.
