Soonex starts as a **static Tableau** site. Mix, Tailwind, and esbuild are already in the project. There is no `package.json`, no Lenis, and no LiveView pager.

The first pass put a waitlist on the page and a countdown aimed at **1 September**. Copy and stills are placeholders you replace after the fork.

## What we kept upstream

Corex owns the controls. Host CSS retunes layout. Theme overlays may set seeds, colors, radius, fonts, and typography — not scale, duration, or opacity.

### Lists

- Clone, then `mix setup` and `mix soonex.server`
- Waitlist fields are already named
- The header timer appears after the hero

1. Fork the repo
2. Swap the lockup and stills
3. Point the countdown at your date

> The clone strip on Product is the same git URL you run locally.

> [!NOTE]
> This demo waitlist does not collect addresses. Submit still shows the launch toast so you can see the success path.
